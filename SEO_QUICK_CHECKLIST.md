**Q: Why is indexing taking so long?**
A: Normal. Google crawls new sites on a schedule. GSC helps speed this up via manual requests.

**Q: Do I need to pay for anything?**
A: No. Everything is 100% free. Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Google Business Profile are all free forever.

**Q: What if my ranking drops?**
A: Normal fluctuation. Monitor GSC for algorithm changes. If rankings drop significantly, check for crawl errors or manual actions.

**Q: Should I buy backlinks?**
A: No. Violates Google's guidelines. Focus on creating great content — backlinks come naturally.
